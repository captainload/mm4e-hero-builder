M&M 4E Hero Builder Manual & Tab Guide

Detailed guide to builder tabs, power point budgeting, mechanics & rules
🌟 Overview & Power Point Economy
Core Engine

Welcome to the Mutants & Masterminds 4th Edition (4E) Hero Builder! This application gives you a fast, rules-accurate, and fully interactive workshop for creating superheroes, villains, minions, mecha, and alternate forms.

  • Power Level (PL): The global benchmark of your campaign (default PL 10). Controls total starting Power Points (PL × 15) and maximum defense/attack caps.
  • Hero Points: In 4E, starting Hero Points are calculated automatically as 1 + Luck advantage ranks. Used during play to reroll d20 checks, counter effects, or perform heroic extra effort.
  • Top Summary Bar: Displays real-time point expenditures across Abilities, Skills, Advantages, Powers, and Defenses, and warns you whenever you exceed your total allowed Power Points.
💡 Pro-Tip: All calculations update in real time as you adjust ranks, advantages, or modifiers. The active editing context can be switched at any time between your Primary Hero and any companion, mecha, or alternate form using the top header dropdown.
🦸 Basics & Stats Tab
Tab 1: tab-basics

The foundation of your character's physical, mental, and defensive capabilities.

  • Hero & Player Info: Set Character Name, Player Name, Power Level, Archetype, and Point Budget multiplier.
  • The 8 Core Abilities (2 PP per rank):
    • STR (Strength): Lifting, close damage, Athletics checks.
    • STA (Stamina): Health, natural Toughness, Fortitude baseline.
    • AGI (Agility): Body control, Dodge defense, Stealth, Acrobatics, Initiative.
    • DEX (Dexterity): Hand-eye coordination, ranged attack checks, Sleight of Hand, Vehicles.
    • FGT (Fighting): Close combat attack bonuses, Parry defense baseline.
    • INT (Intellect): Reasoning, Knowledge, Technology, Treatment, Investigation.
    • AWE (Awareness): Perception, Insight, Will defense baseline.
    • PRE (Presence): Personality, Deception, Intimidation, Persuasion.
  • Combat Maneuvers & Unarmed Attack: Displays unarmed attack bonus (FGT + Close Attack) and damage (STR rank). Offers quick reference for tactical combat maneuvers (Power Attack, Defensive Attack, All-Out Attack).
  • Defenses & Resistance Checks:
    • Dodge & Parry: Avoid ranged and melee attacks.
    • Toughness: Resist physical damage (STA rank + Protection + Defensive Roll).
    • Fortitude: Resist toxins, radiation, diseases, fatigue.
    • Will: Resist mental, psychic, and emotional attacks.
⚠️ Power Level Trade-off Limits:
• Dodge + Toughness ≤ 2 × PL and Parry + Toughness ≤ 2 × PL
• Fortitude + Will ≤ 2 × PL
• Attack Bonus + Effect Rank ≤ 2 × PL
The builder automatically validates these pairs and highlights any violations in red.
🎯 Skills Tab
Tab 2: tab-skills

Represents training, specialized expertise, and learned disciplines.

  • Cost: 1 Power Point buys 2 skill ranks (0.5 PP per rank).
  • Total Modifier Calculation: Governing Ability rank + Trained Ranks + Advantage / Enhanced Trait bonuses.
  • Power Level Cap: No skill modifier can exceed PL + 10 (e.g. at PL 10, maximum skill check modifier is +20).
  • Interactive Sorting: Click any table header (Skill Name, Ability, Ranks, Bonus, Total) to toggle ascending/descending order.
💡 Pro-Tip: Untrained skills automatically use their base ability score. Hover over skill names to review their common uses, check types, and trained-only restrictions.
🎖️ Advantages Tab
Tab 3: tab-advantages

Minor special talents, combat tricks, resources, and tactical benefits.

  • Cost: Standard 1 PP per advantage rank.
  • Four Distinct Categories:
    • Combat: Accurate Attack, All-Out Attack, Defensive Roll, Improved Critical, Power Attack, Takedown, Precise Attack.
    • Fortune: Luck (bonus hero points), Seize Initiative, Inspire, Ultimate Effort.
    • General: Benefit (Wealth, Status), Diehard, Eidetic Memory, Equipment, Fearless, Languages, Trance.
    • Skill: Attractive, Daze, Fascinate, Jack-of-All-Trades, Skill Mastery, Well-Informed.
  • Heroic Limits: Multi-rank advantages (e.g. Improved Critical up to 4 ranks, Luck up to PL/2) enforce rules caps automatically.
  • Category & Keyword Search: Use the category filter dropdown and search input to quickly locate specific advantages.
⚡ Powers Tab
Tab 4: tab-powers

The powerhouse of character creation. Create superhuman abilities, technological gadgets, magic spells, and psychic phenomena.

  • Power Containers: Group effects into logical powers. Each container has its own name, descriptors (e.g. Fire, Magic, Tech, Mutant), and structure.
  • Alternate Effect (AE) Arrays: Containers can host alternate powers for just 1 PP each. Only one alternate configuration in an array is active at a time, sharing the highest-cost effect's point budget.
  • Extras & Flaws:
    • Extras: Increase effect potency (+1, +2 per rank, or flat +1 PP). Examples: Multiattack, Increased Range, Area Effect.
    • Flaws: Limit or restrict an effect (-1, -2 per rank, or flat -1 PP). Examples: Distracting, Limited, Tiring, Removable.
    • Fractional Cost Rule: If flaws exceed base cost, cost becomes 1 PP per 2, 3, 4, or 5 ranks.
  • ⚙️ Power Configurations: Effects with specialized options show a ⚙️ Has Configs indicator. Click to configure:
    • Affliction: Choose 1st, 2nd, and 3rd degree conditions (Dazed, Stunned, Incapacitated, Transformed).
    • Enhanced Trait: Select which abilities, skills, or advantages this power enhances.
    • Senses: Pick accurate, acute, analytical, darkvision, or penetrating senses.
    • Compound Presets: One-click multi-effect powers like Absorption, Berserker Rage, and Poltergeist.
💡 Pro-Tip: The builder dynamically tracks the base cost per rank, active modifiers, and total container cost in real time.
🛡️ Equipment & HQ Tab
Tab 5: tab-equipment

Manage mortal gear, weapons, body armor, vehicles, and super-headquarters.

  • Equipment Point (EP) Economy: 1 Power Point invested in the Equipment advantage grants 5 Equipment Points (EP).
  • Equipment vs. Devices: Equipment is mundane, commercially available technology (can be taken away, run out of ammo, or break). Inherent superhero gadgets and indestructible devices should be built in the Powers tab with the Removable flaw.
  • Personal Gear: Weapons (handguns, swords, energy blasters), tactical armor, commlinks, night vision goggles.
  • Vehicles: Build cars, motorcycles, jets, and starships with custom size categories, strength, speed, toughness, defense, and vehicular weapons.
  • Headquarters: Design secret sanctums, orbital watchtowers, and underground caves with size, toughness, and features (Computers, Defense Systems, Laboratories, Infirmaries, Teleport Pads).
🤖 Companions & Alternate Forms Tab
Tab 6: tab-companions

Full support for secondary characters, allied summons, piloted mecha, and transformative identities.

  • Minions (1 PP per 15 PP minion): Faithful side-characters, henchmen, automated drones, or animal companions. Subject to standard M&M minion rules (cannot score critical hits, fail resistance checks against damage on a single degree of failure).
  • Mecha: Giant piloted suits and robot allies with their own equipment frameworks, armor, and cockpits.
  • Alternate Forms & Metamorph: Characters with dual identities (e.g. Bruce Banner / The Hulk, Billy Batson / Shazam, civilian / armored battle-form).
  • Seamless Form Switching: Use the Active Form dropdown in the top header bar to instantly swap between your Primary Hero and any companion. The entire builder UI updates to edit that specific form's sheet!
📐 Improv Builder / Blueprints Tab
Tab 7: tab-blueprints

An isolated testing workbench and blueprint scratchpad for power stunt design.

  • Isolated Scratchpad: Prototype complex compound powers, calculate modifier math, and experiment with novel effect combinations without cluttering your hero's active power list.
  • Instant Stunt Math: Quickly verify if an alternate effect stunt fits within your existing power array's point budget during a session.
🎭 Background & Complications Tab
Tab 8: tab-background

The roleplaying heart and narrative engine of your character.

  • Motivations: Why does your character put their life on the line? (Justice, Doing Good, Responsibility, Greed, Thrills, Acceptance).
  • Complications: The primary source of Hero Points in Mutants & Masterminds! Whenever a complication impacts your character negatively in a scene, your GM awards you a Hero Point.
    Examples: Secret Identity, Enemy, Phobia, Vulnerability, Relationship, Reputation, Disability, Code of Honor.
  • Dossier Details: Real name, secret vs. public identity, height, weight, eyes, hair, costume description, group affiliations, and personal backstory.
